# Technical textbook sample: Steel Structure Design Principles

## Source handling

- The source is a privately supplied scanned textbook used only for layout observation.
- Source pages, OCR output, extracted images, and local file paths are not committed.
- The scan contains 501 page images. Scan canvas size, crop, skew, and outer white space are not authoritative page geometry.
- The copyright opening identifies a 720 x 1000 mm 1/16 printing format, which is evidence about the printed edition, not a direct DOCX page-size instruction.

## Reliable observations

- The book uses a conventional engineering-textbook sequence: title and copyright matter, prefaces, contents, chapters, chapter-end material, references, appendices, and a back index.
- Chapter titles form the strongest body hierarchy. Lower headings use decimal numbering.
- Figures, tables, and displayed equations are numbered by chapter with a dot separator.
- Figure captions appear below figures; table titles appear above tables.
- Displayed equations are centered and their numbers align toward the right margin.
- Running headers and page numbers use mirrored left/right-page behavior.
- Chapter-summary or teaching-emphasis content is visually separated from ordinary body text.
- The back index is set in two columns and preserves stroke-based grouping.
- The visible content block is broadly stable relative to the page, supporting proportional-margin candidates, but not exact millimetre recovery.

## Cross-sample synthesis

Together with the third edition of *Steel Structure Fundamentals*, this sample supports a shared technical-textbook profile for body hierarchy, chapter-based numbering, captions, equations, mirrored running elements, exercises or teaching aids, references, appendices, and dense technical tables.

Differences between the books are evidence to report, not permission to silently select one publisher's treatment. A caller's explicit current-task requirements and QA decisions have the highest formatting authority.

## Not established by this sample

The reviewed pages do not establish exact DOCX fonts, point sizes, line spacing, section-break behavior, MathType object handling, footnote or endnote style, long quotations, answer layout, or complex teaching-box construction. These remain candidate or pending-QA rules.

The scan does not make formula images acceptable. Every delivered formula must remain editable as OMML or a supported editable equation object; LaTeX input must become editable OMML rather than a raster image.
